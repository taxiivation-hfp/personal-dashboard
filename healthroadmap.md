# Your plan

Ordered by ratio of visible return to cost and risk. Work down at whatever pace suits. Every tier is a complete place to stop.

---

## Tier 1 — Do now, no gatekeeping
**~AUD 1,600–3,600 · self-managed**

**Skin**
- AM: gentle cleanser → azelaic acid 15–20% → moisturiser → SPF 50
- PM: gentle cleanser → tretinoin 0.025%, 3 nights/week building up → moisturiser
- Add nothing else for 3 months. Expect worse before better, 6–10 weeks. Judge at 6 months.
- Skip: GHK-Cu, estriol, hydroquinone for now

**Dental**
- Clean, restore the decay, then whiten. Prerequisite for any ortho later anyway.

**Hair**
- A cut that exposes the forehead and doesn't add width at the jaw. Your current one hides the structure you have.

**Neck and traps**
- Direct neck work plus shrugs. Won't build a jawline but reduces how much the undefined one reads as a gap.

**Sleep hygiene**
- Consistent bedtime, dark room, no screens late. Free, and it's the input to the puffiness you've described.

---

## Tier 2 — Diagnostics
**~AUD 1,600–3,900 · unlocks everything below**

- Large-FOV CBCT + lateral and PA ceph
- TMJ MRI
- Sleep study
- ENT with nasal endoscopy — your "fine now" plus fatigue-from-talking needs an actual answer
- Orthodontist consult, surgical-capable
- Basic bloods: iron, thyroid, vitamin D
- Photos and the five measurements

Nothing below this line can be sensibly planned without these numbers.

---

## Tier 3 — Cheap functional wins
**~AUD 600–1,500**

- ENT-directed nasal treatment if the scope finds something
- Respiratory physio for breathing pattern, if the fatigue isn't explained by the above
- Treat whatever the bloods and sleep study turn up

Some of your under-eye darkness and facial puffiness may resolve here for a few hundred dollars. Worth knowing before attributing it to bone.

---

## Tier 4 — Reversible tests
**~AUD 1,600–4,500 · optional**

- Masseter botox trial — tells you in 6 weeks whether muscle contributes to the cheek width
- HA filler to malar and chin — tests whether added projection improves your look before implant money
- Skip both if you're already committed to the bimax

---

## Tier 5 — MARPE
**~AUD 2,500–6,000 · only if CBCT confirms transverse deficiency**

The one surgical item defensible standalone, because the airway benefit holds even if you stop here. Diastema opens temporarily. Do it for the airway and the smile width, not for FWHR.

---

## Tier 6 — Bimax
**~AUD 50,000–85,000 Seoul · 30,000–55,000 Jakarta/Bangkok · ~30 months**

Decompensation ortho → surgery → finishing ortho. One operation: Le Fort I + BSSO + genioplasty + septoplasty + custom hardware. 3–4 weeks in country.

Fixes the cant, midline shift, gum display, chin projection, chin-throat angle, central midface, occlusal plane, lip incompetence, airway.

The only all-or-nothing decision here. Everything above is incremental; this isn't.

---

## Tier 7 — Reassess
**~AUD 350–650 · 9–12 months post-op**

Fresh photos and CT once soft tissue settles. Then decide on Tier 8. A lot of people don't want it by this point.

---

## Tier 8 — Second stage
**~AUD 20,000–45,000 · locked behind Tier 6**

- Custom malar / infraorbital implants
- Crown lengthening
- Rhinoplasty
- Buccal fat removal, only if still indicated
- Plate removal

Doing any of these before the bimax means paying for them twice.

---

## Rules that hold across the whole plan

- **Tier 2 before any surgical decision.** Under 4k and it determines which of Tiers 5, 6 and 8 actually apply to you.
- **Nothing from Tier 8 before Tier 6.** Only real sequencing trap on the list.
- **Never:** Le Fort II/III, buccal fat removal now, masseter reduction, topical estriol.
- **One variable at a time.** Start five things at once and you'll never know which one worked.
