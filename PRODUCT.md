# Personal Dashboard

**Register:** product. Design SERVES the task. The tool should disappear into the day.

`CLAUDE.md` is the constitution and outranks this file and any design skill. Where a
design convention and CLAUDE.md disagree, CLAUDE.md wins. This document exists so design
tooling has the context; it does not restate the rules, it points at them.

## What it is

A private, single-user daily guide organised around **adherence, not accomplishment**. It
answers "did I do what I said I'd do?" — never "how much did I get done?" The problem
being solved is drift and self-justification, not productivity.

One user. One device pair (a laptop and an iPhone). Used several times a day, every day,
for months. That frequency is the single most important design fact: **anything seen
dozens of times a day must not be animated, decorated, or clever.**

## Who, where, under what light

A student at a desk in Melbourne, opening this between classes and again at 11pm in bed
with the lights off, to answer one question and mark two or three things. Never presented,
never shown to anyone, never used to impress. It is closer to a bathroom scale than to a
dashboard: you look, you learn one thing, you leave.

That sentence forces the theme. **Light, warm, quiet.** A dark "tool" theme would be
category reflex — productivity app, therefore dark, therefore cool grey — and it would
also be wrong for a thing you read in daylight far more often than at night.

## Color strategy

**Restrained**, and committed to. Warm beige page, off-white surfaces, one muted amber
accent, ink at 13.9:1. Three desaturated pillar hues exist strictly as categorisation and
never as status.

The beige is NOT the default warm-neutral reflex — it predates this work, it is a
deliberate identity, and CLAUDE.md names it. Identity preservation wins.

**Fixed and not editable:** accent, ink, surfaces, and the three block states. The block
states deliberately avoid a severity ramp and avoid red entirely, because marking
"drifted" must never feel punishing. **Editable:** pillar hues only, from a curated
palette. Do not add a free hex picker.

## Typography

One family, two weights only — 400 and 600. **No 500.** The restraint is the design, and a
third weight is a bug. Fixed rem scale, not fluid: this is product UI at consistent DPI.

## Motion

Minimal and functional. State, feedback, and reveal only. Nothing choreographed, no page
load sequence, no decorative delight. The hero number changes several times a day; it must
never make the user wait to read it.

## The hierarchy that must survive any redesign

1. **Today's adherence** — the single largest thing on the screen.
2. The day's blocks and non-negotiables — what you actually tap.
3. Pillar rings — secondary context, a balance indicator, never the hero.
4. Goals and skills — one tap deeper in spirit, present but quiet.

## Non-goals

No gamification of any kind. No XP, levels, points, progression, or streak mechanics
beyond a plain count. No output measurement. No comparison between days or fortnights. A
thin ring means neglected, never failed.
